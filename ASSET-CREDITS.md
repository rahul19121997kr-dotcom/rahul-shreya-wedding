# Artwork credit

The Ganesha image in `index.html` and `wedding-invitation.mp4` is a reproduction of a Nurpur school miniature of four-armed Ganesha, circa 1810, from Wikimedia Commons. The Commons file page identifies it as a faithful reproduction of a public-domain artwork and carries the Public Domain Mark:

https://commons.wikimedia.org/wiki/File:Ganesha_Nurpur_miniature_circa_1810_Dubost_p64.jpg

The marigold, jasmine, and leaf-vine artwork in `assets/floral-vine.svg` was drawn for this invitation.

The audio is an original instrumental melody synthesized for this invitation. The on-screen wedding couplet is original text.
